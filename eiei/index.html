Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the fall risk assessment?

Our fall risk assessment is based on advanced AI algorithms that analyze your walking pattern, balance, and other factors. While it provides a good indication of your fall risk, it should be used as a guide and not as a replacement for professional medical advice.

How often should I upload walking videos?

We recommend uploading a walking video at least once a week for regular monitoring. If you've made changes to your exercise routine or experienced any changes in your walking ability, you may want to upload more frequently.

Can I share my results with my doctor?

Yes, you can share your fall risk assessment and stability analysis with your healthcare provider. Use the "Share with Doctor" button on the Fall Risk page to generate a report that you can download or email directly to your doctor.
